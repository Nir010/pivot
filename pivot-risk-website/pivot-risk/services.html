Our Services

We help organizations carry risk

We provide integrated actuarial, risk management, insurance, engineering valuation and financial advisory services designed to help organizations understand risk, strengthen decision making and improve financial resilience. Our services combine technical expertise, analytical modelling, industry experience and practical advisory support to address the specific requirements of our clients.

Actuarial Valuation and Risk Assessment

We check the financial health of insurance companies, help them price their products correctly, and assess their risks, so they stay strong and prepared.

Employee Benefits Valuation

We work out how much money a company needs to set aside today for the benefits it has promised its staffs, like gratuity, leave and pension.

Risk Management Services

We help businesses spot risks early and plan ahead so small problems don't turn into big losses later.

Reinsurance Risk Analytics

We help insurance companies share very big risks with other insurers, so one disaster doesn't hurt them badly.

Engineering Valuation and Assessment

We find out the true worth of houses, buildings, bridges and factories, useful for insurance and smart business decisions.

Loss Assessment and Survey Services

After an accident or damage, we inspect and assess the loss so insurance claims are settled fairly and correctly.

Financial Services

We help businesses plan their capital and understand how their decisions will affect them financially.

Insurance and Reinsurance Advisory

We give simple, practical advice on insurance from choosing the right cover to handling claims, so businesses pay the right price for the right protection.
